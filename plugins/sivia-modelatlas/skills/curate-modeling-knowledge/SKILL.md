# Curate Modeling Knowledge

Search with `atlas_find_papers` or the available AnySearch skill, favoring problem-specific studies, official problems and directly relevant visualization methods. Search results enter the library as `discovered`; inspect the original source before assigning meaning or awards. Paper text imports remain `text_imported` until a reviewed card is created.

Read the source figure, caption and surrounding interpretation together when building a visual case. A card must record `id`, `title`, `figure_kind`, `claim`, `source_ids`, `locator`, `tags`, `guidance`, and optionally `example` and `competition`. Register unknown source IDs before `atlas_add_card`. The locator identifies the actual page, section or figure supporting the note. Distinguish the paper's method from our own proposed adaptation.

Store an original rendered case as a bundle linking the complete spec/prompt, actual image, source note, data snapshot and audit. A paper link or unexecuted prompt is a reference card, not a completed drawing case. Do not upload third-party PDFs, source figures, user datasets or local caches without an applicable redistribution basis.

Primary collection: MCM/ICM figures and data-modeling plots. CUMCM is an optional transfer profile. Avoid expanding into generic paper writing or autonomously launching competition experiments when the user asked for figure knowledge.
