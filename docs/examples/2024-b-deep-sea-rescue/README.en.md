## Caption

Conceptual drift-to-search overview. Current forecasting and spatial regression of currents and density support a three-dimensional passive-motion prediction constrained above the seabed. Its horizontal location centers an assumed Manhattan-distance Poisson-shell search field. Two maps preserve the same coordinates: an unsuccessful parallel sweep adds D4 to the excluded set, while a later predicted position supplies the adjacent center and the next search assignment. Permanent exclusion depends on the paper's perfect-detection and no-reentry approximations; assignments change only between intervals and inter-cell transit is neglected. Cells and shading illustrate the mechanism rather than computed probabilities; the approximately 300 m cell and 30 min interval are the paper's example assumptions.
