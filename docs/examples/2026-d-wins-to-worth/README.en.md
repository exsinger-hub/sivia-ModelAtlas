## Caption

Conceptual overview of the paper's sports-franchise decision mechanism. A finite seat capacity censors observed attendance, so expected admissions are computed as E[min(demand, capacity) | features] before being combined with a distinct ticket-pricing layer. Ticket and other revenues enter an explicit operating ledger; profit, borrowing and repayment update separate cash and debt stocks. Distinct valuation lenses inform basis-dependent franchise value and leverage, while roster increments and feasibility constraints are re-evaluated with newly available segment information. All scene geometry is schematic. Pricing, valuation aggregation and injury-process ambiguities in the manuscript remain unresolved rather than being converted into numerical claims.
